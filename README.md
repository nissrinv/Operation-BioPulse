# Project Code Name: Operation Bio-Pulse

## Background Briefing
You have just been brought in as the **Lead Data Analyst** for a specialised medical research unit. The team recently concluded a phase trial tracking a new experimental treatment, but the raw data has just landed on your desk in three separate silos (`demographics`, `vitals`, and `adverse_events`). 

Rumour has it the data is messy, riddled with anomalies, and hiding crucial safety signals that could make or break the trial's approval.

## Your Mission
Before the executive board meeting, you must dive into the raw files to clean, merge, and interrogate the data to uncover the truth. Check your `answers/answer_key.txt` only when you believe you have cracked the case.

## Dataset Manifest
* **`data/demographics.csv`**: Patient background profiles and baseline metadata.
* **`data/vitals.csv`**: Time-series or recorded vital sign measurements during the trial.
* **`data/adverse_events.csv`**: Logged incidents, side effects, and clinical reactions.
